St. Xavier's High School is a private, mixed-day school owned and managed by the Catholic Diocese of Nakuru in collaboration with the Incarnate Word Sisters.

The school offers holistic education that provides intellectual, spiritual, moral and social growth, founded on Christian values and principles.
